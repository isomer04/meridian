## II.A.1.b — Eligibility for FHA-insured financing

The borrower must have a valid Social Security number, be an owner-occupant of the subject
property, and meet the citizenship or lawful residency requirements. FHA financing is available
only for a principal residence; second homes and investment properties are not eligible.

## II.A.2.a — Maximum mortgage amounts and loan limits

The maximum mortgage is the lesser of the statutory loan limit for the area and the applicable
loan-to-value limit applied to the adjusted value. The adjusted value is the lesser of the
purchase price less any inducements to purchase, or the appraised value.

## II.A.2.b — Loan-to-value limits

The maximum LTV is 96.5 percent for a borrower with a minimum decision credit score of 580 or
above. A borrower with a score between 500 and 579 is limited to 90 percent. A borrower with a
score below 500 is not eligible.

## II.A.4.b — Minimum decision credit score

The minimum decision credit score is the middle of the three scores where three are returned, or
the lower of two where only two are returned. On a loan with multiple borrowers the lowest
minimum decision credit score among the borrowers applies.

## II.A.4.c — Evaluating credit history

A borrower with a bankruptcy discharged less than two years prior, or a foreclosure completed
less than three years prior, requires documentation of extenuating circumstances. A borrower
delinquent on federal debt is not eligible until the delinquency is resolved.

## II.A.5.a — Income requirements — effective income

Effective income must be reasonably likely to continue for at least three years. Overtime and
bonus income may be used where it has been received for at least two years and is reasonably
likely to continue. A borrower employed for less than two years requires documentation of prior
schooling or training in the same field.

## II.A.5.b — Self-employment income

A borrower self-employed for at least two years may use the average of the two most recent years.
A borrower self-employed between one and two years may qualify only where the borrower was
previously employed in the same line of work and the income shows stability. Business tax
returns and a year-to-date profit and loss statement are required.

## II.A.5.d — Qualifying ratios — manual underwriting

For a manually underwritten loan the maximum mortgage payment expense to effective income ratio
is 31 percent and the maximum total fixed payment to effective income ratio is 43 percent. Ratios
above these thresholds require documented compensating factors, and the maximum total ratio may
not exceed 50 percent under any circumstances.

## II.A.6.a — Mortgage insurance premiums

FHA requires both an upfront mortgage insurance premium and an annual premium collected monthly.
For most loans with an LTV above 90 percent the annual premium is payable for the full term of
the mortgage rather than falling away at a specified LTV.

## II.A.7.a — Appraisal requirements

An FHA appraisal is required on every case and must be performed by an appraiser on the FHA
roster. FHA does not offer a value acceptance or appraisal waiver equivalent; the property must be
appraised and must meet the minimum property requirements.

## II.A.8.a — Property acceptability criteria

The property must be free of conditions that affect the health and safety of the occupants or the
structural soundness of the improvements. Deficiencies noted by the appraiser must be repaired
and the repairs verified before the mortgage may be insured.
